## About Zupper

[Zupper](https://zupper.co/) is a B2B e-commerce platform built to help businesses discover products, connect with suppliers, and manage their wholesale purchasing needs online.

We aim to make business-to-business commerce simpler, more accessible, and more efficient for buyers and sellers.

## What we do

- B2B product discovery and online purchasing
- Wholesale commerce for businesses
- Supplier and buyer connections
- Streamlined ordering for business customers
- Digital solutions for modern commerce
