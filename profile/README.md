## About Zupper

[Zupper](https://zupper.co/) is a B2B e-commerce platform from Bengaluru, India. We help businesses discover products, connect with suppliers, and manage wholesale purchasing through a convenient digital commerce experience.

Our goal is to make business buying more accessible, transparent, and efficient for the growing Indian business community.

## What we are building

- 🛒 Simple online product discovery for businesses
- 🤝 Better connections between buyers and suppliers
- 📦 Convenient wholesale purchasing and ordering
- ⚡ Digital tools that reduce friction in procurement
- 📈 Commerce experiences that help businesses grow

## Technology at Zupper

Our products combine practical engineering with a strong focus on customer experience. The exact stack can evolve as the platform grows, but these are the fundamentals we value:

### Frontend basics

- HTML, CSS, and responsive design
- JavaScript and modern browser APIs
- React and component-based UI development
- Accessibility, performance, and reusable design systems

### Backend and APIs

- REST APIs and service-oriented thinking
- Authentication, authorization, and secure data handling
- Relational databases, data modelling, and SQL
- Validation, error handling, logging, and testing

### Platform and engineering

- Git and GitHub collaboration
- Code reviews and clear documentation
- CI/CD and automated quality checks
- Cloud deployment, monitoring, and reliability
- Security, privacy, and scalable architecture

### Product and commerce knowledge

- Product catalogues and search
- Cart, checkout, orders, and payments
- Inventory, pricing, and supplier workflows
- Customer support and business operations

## Learn with us and work with us

Interested in building products that support real businesses? Start with the basics, build consistently, and share what you learn.

### A good learning path

1. Learn HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git, and GitHub.
2. Build a responsive product catalogue and search page.
3. Learn React and create reusable components.
4. Learn APIs, authentication, SQL, and database design.
5. Build a small cart, order, or inventory project.
6. Add tests, documentation, CI/CD, and deployment.
7. Read our repositories, open issues, and share improvements.

### Who we welcome

- Students and self-taught developers
- Frontend, backend, full-stack, and mobile engineers
- QA, DevOps, product, design, and data professionals
- People curious about B2B commerce and Indian businesses
- Contributors who value learning, ownership, and teamwork

If you are learning and would like to connect, explore our GitHub projects and reach out to us. We are always interested in meeting people who want to learn, build, and grow with Zupper.
